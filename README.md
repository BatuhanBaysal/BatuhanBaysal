### 🚀 Featured Projects

My projects are built on **Java and Spring Boot** (backend), **React and TypeScript** (frontend), **PostgreSQL and Redis** (data) and **Docker**. I plan the work with milestones and issues and ship it through pull requests and tagged releases.

#### [ticket-reservation-engine](https://github.com/BatuhanBaysal/ticket-reservation-engine)
*Nov 2026 – Present · In progress*
`Kafka` `Saga` `Transactional Outbox`

- **Why:** To learn how separate services stay consistent when something fails halfway.
- **Problems:** Two people booking the same seat during a traffic spike, and undoing a reservation safely when a payment fails.
- **Approach:** Order, inventory and payment services, with the Saga pattern handling rollbacks.

#### [chess-platform](https://github.com/BatuhanBaysal/chess-platform)
*Mar 2026 – Oct 2026*
`RabbitMQ` `WebSocket` `Keycloak`

- **Why:** To see what happens to a real-time app when many people act at the same time.
- **Problems:** Two players moving or joining a room at the same moment, and slow game analysis freezing live games.
- **Solution:** My own chess rule engine, locks to handle simultaneous actions one at a time, and Stockfish analysis moved to background workers.
